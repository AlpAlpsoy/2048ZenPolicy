2048 Zen

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 10, 2026

2048 Zen ("the app") is developed by an independent developer ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and your choices. This app is free to download and is supported by advertising.

Information we collect

2048 Zen does not require an account, a sign-up, or a login. We do not collect your name, email, or any personal profile information.

  • High score: stored only on your device (via iOS UserDefaults). It is never transmitted to us or to any server we control.
  • Advertising data: the app displays ads served by Google AdMob (see below).

Advertising (Google AdMob)

We use Google AdMob to show banner and interstitial ads. To serve and measure ads, Google's SDK may collect technical and device information such as device identifiers, general device/OS information, IP-derived approximate location, and ad interaction data (e.g. impressions, clicks).

This version of the app does not request App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission, so ads are served without access to your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) for cross-app tracking. Google may still use limited, non-tracking signals (such as general device and contextual information) to serve ads.

Google's own use of data is governed by their policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google's Ad Partner policy

Children's privacy

2048 Zen is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app displays ads, parental guidance is recommended for younger players.

Data sharing

We do not sell your data. The only data shared with a third party is the advertising/device data described above, shared with Google solely for the purpose of serving and measuring ads within the app.

Your choices

  • You can limit ad tracking at any time via iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising, or Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • You can review and adjust Google's ad personalization settings at adssettings.google.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves (for example, if we add new features or ad formats). Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email alp.studio@yahoo.com.

Uygulamanın bu sürümü App Tracking Transparency (ATT) izni istemiyor, bu yüzden reklamlar cihazının reklam kimliğine (IDFA) erişilmeden, uygulamalar arası takip olmadan sunuluyor. Google yine de reklam sunmak için sınırlı, takip amacı taşımayan sinyaller (genel cihaz ve bağlamsal bilgi gibi) kullanabilir.
